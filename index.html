Understanding the Koopman Operator

The Shadow Analogy

Imagine a 3D object casting a 2D shadow. The shadow moves in complex, nonlinear ways. But if you know the shadow is from a simple rotating sphere, you can predict its motion linearly!

The Key Insight:

🔵 The shadow (2D) moves nonlinearly

🌐 The sphere (3D) rotates linearly

✨ By "lifting" to 3D, we see the linear pattern!

Mathematical Framework

Koopman Operator Definition:

$$ \mathcal{K}f(\xi(t), \zeta(t)) = \frac{\partial f}{\partial \xi}\frac{d\xi}{dt} = \frac{\partial f}{\partial \xi}F(\xi(t), \zeta(t)) $$

Bilinear Form:

$$ \dot{\tilde{\xi}} = \tilde{A}\tilde{\xi} + \tilde{B}\tilde{\zeta} + \sum_{j=1}^{\tilde{n}}\tilde{\xi}_j \tilde{N}_j \tilde{\zeta} $$

The last term captures control-affine dynamics!

Linearization Strategy:

$$ \dot{\tilde{\xi}} = \bar{A}\tilde{\xi} + \bar{B}\tilde{\zeta} = \tilde{A}\tilde{\xi} + \left[\tilde{B} + \sum_{j=1}^{\tilde{n}}\tilde{\xi}_j(0)\tilde{N}_j\right]\tilde{\zeta} $$

Freeze coupling states at t=0 in each receding horizon

Glossary & References

Koopman Operator

A mathematical operator that lifts nonlinear dynamics to a higher-dimensional space where they evolve linearly.

EDMD

Extended Dynamic Mode Decomposition - algorithm for approximating the Koopman operator from data.

Bilinear Systems

Systems with dynamics of the form: ẋ = Ax + Bu + ΣxjNju, capturing state-control coupling.

Control-Affine Dynamics

Terms in the dynamics where states and controls are multiplied together, representing their coupling.

MPC

Model Predictive Control - optimization-based control that predicts future states and optimizes control inputs.

Bicycle Model

Simplified vehicle model treating front and rear axles as single wheels, balancing fidelity and simplicity.

Lifting Function (Φ)

Function that maps original states to higher-dimensional observable space.

Drivable Tube

Collision-free corridor defined for vehicle center of gravity to ensure safe trajectories.
